# Draft Quality Checklist

Must pass before delivery:

- Hook: the opening states the reader's problem or benefit.
- Coherence: each section supports one claim and transitions naturally.
- Evidence: strong claims have concrete cases, data, scenarios, or explicit uncertainty.
- DNA fit: instruction is executed and evaluation score is at least 80.
- WeChat readability: paragraph and sentence behavior follow the selected DNA.
- CTA: the ending has one relevant action.
- Source handling: referenced facts and user-provided viewpoints are represented accurately.

Anti-patterns:

- Empty motivational cliches.
- Abstract nouns without examples.
- Repetitive sentence openings.
- Exaggerated promises or unverifiable superlatives.

Separate reviews:

- Fact checking.
- Platform compliance.
- Typography preview.
- `content-calibrator` score when requested.
